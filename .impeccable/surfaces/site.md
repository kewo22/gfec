## Direction contract

THESIS: Refuses both the glossy stock-photo "global education" hero and the tailor's-ledger world already built on the sibling branch — instead borrows the one document every visitor here has actually held and feared: the Sri Lankan O/L or A/L exam result slip. The site reads like your file has already been examined, graded, and officially sealed — pass, not glossy hope.

OWN-WORLD: Government-examinations paper world. Pale gazette-cream ground, exam-ink black rules, carbon-typewriter monospace numerals for grades and stats, a red-and-gold circular seal/stamp motif for verification and CTA states, index-number referencing (testimonials, universities, destinations each carry a ledger-style index number, like an admission number). One committed exam-authority green as the accent, carried at section scale, never a decorative sprinkle. Raised 3x by declined challengers from the direction round: ballistic raise (stat digits flip with real mechanical weight, not a generic count-up), flat-layer raise (depth from overlapping flat color, never gradients or drop-shadows), plain-sequence raise (the journey section teaches one idea per step, no dense paragraphs).

STORY: A visitor lands and reads "your file already passes," browses destination "subjects" the way a mark sheet lists subjects and grades, follows the numbered Discover→Fly journey the way a results table reads top to bottom, sees one testimonial as a fully graded and sealed result, and reaches the consultation form feeling like formal registration, not a lead-gen funnel.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed gazette-cream hero, black rule lines top and bottom under the sticky header, a large seal/stamp graphic near the headline, headline set as a graded verdict ("You. Qualified. Approved."), subhead, primary CTA styled as an official registration action; a floating mark-sheet card shows the real trust-stat strip as a graded subject table (each stat carries an index number and a green PASS tick) — replacing the sibling branch's ledger-card panel.

FORM: The Result Slip world — Impeccable's Pick, index 1 of 7 self-derived grounded directions (ranked: exam result slip [chosen] > airport send-off/departure hall > university offer letter & crest > passport & visa stamp collage > hand-painted bus/rail destination board > bank guarantee/forex counter [dice-assigned, not chosen] > doorstep blessing pattern), locked over the dice-assigned bank-guarantee/forex-counter direction. Honest risk disclosed at pick time: exam-slip iconography carries real anxiety association (results day is genuinely stressful for this audience) — mitigated by keeping tone at "you already passed" reassurance, seal language celebratory (green PASS, gold seal), never punitive.
