Audience/job/action/proof/constraints: Sri Lankan A/L-age students and their parents evaluating whether GFEC is a real, trustworthy, locally-present consultancy worth handing their study-abroad process to. Proof: real trust stats (14 countries, 600+ universities, 150+ courses, 30+ students, "since 2021"), 2 real testimonials, 23 real named universities, real gallery photos. Constraint: must preserve every existing form/API/URL/content fact (see PRODUCT.md); user's own brief pins the color and type palette already (Deep Navy/Midnight Blue primary, Royal Blue secondary, Warm Gold/Amber accent, off-white background, dark charcoal text; Manrope/Inter/Plus Jakarta Sans/DM Sans typography shortlist) — this direction translates its material into that pinned palette rather than overriding it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Refuses both the glossy stock-photo "global education" hero and the flat corporate-blue brochure site — instead borrows the bespoke tailor's ledger: every family's study-abroad file feels personally measured, recorded, and stitched together by one hand, not auto-generated from a template.

OWN-WORLD: Deep navy grounds with off-white ledger-panel cards; warm-gold thread-stitch dividers and ribbon-seal accents (translating the source world's indigo-thread/kraft-paper materials into the brief's pinned navy/royal-blue/gold/off-white/charcoal palette); ruled ledger-line tables and grids for facts/comparisons; tape-measure tick-mark progress for the student journey timeline; fabric-swatch cards for destination selection; deckle-edge/letterpress touches on offer-letter and CTA moments. Display face Plus Jakarta Sans, body Manrope.

STORY: A visitor lands and immediately reads "your file is already being personally handled here," browses country "swatches," follows a stitched numbered student journey (Discover→Fly), reads one real testimonial prominently, and lands on the consultation form feeling this is a serious hand-run practice, not a lead-gen funnel.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed navy hero, gold thread-stitch rule under the sticky header; headline + subhead + gold-stitched primary CTA left-aligned; an off-white "ledger card" floats right showing the real trust-stat strip (countries/universities/courses/students) styled as ruled ledger entries.

FORM: Tailor's-ledger world — index 5 of 7 self-derived grounded directions (ranked: university acceptance letter/crest > passport & visa stamps > exam admit-card slip > school atlas > tailor's ledger [assigned] > departure-gate send-off > airline boarding pass), raised with the dealt skate-deck challenger's full color commitment (commit fully where accent color is used, no timidity) and the dealt ANSI-BBS challenger's precise monospace state-labeling (ledger reference numbers/status codes set in a monospace numeral style); the dealt jet-age ticket-wallet and paper-automata challengers stay competitive alternates (status-as-stamped-coupon-state for application/journey steps; hand-cranked procession motion for the journey section) available on request.
